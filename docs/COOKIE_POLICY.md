# Proti IQ Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** November 21, 2026  

---

### 1. Essential Functional Cookies Only
Proti IQ strictly uses essential functional cookies necessary to maintain active user session authentication and lock the resolved RBAC persona across page refreshes.

### 2. Cookie Inventory
- `proti_session`: Secure, httpOnly/SameSite token storing the 24-hour signed session JWT. Contains no sensitive personal credentials.
- `proti_cookie_consent`: Local flag confirming acceptance of essential session cookies.

### 3. Zero Third-Party or Advertising Cookies
Proti IQ contains:
- Zero advertising cookies.
- Zero social media tracking pixels.
- Zero analytics telemetry beacons.
- Zero cross-site trackers.
