# Proti IQ — Data Sources & Attributions

All biological and structural databases queried by Proti IQ are open, publicly accessible, and attributed under their respective open-access licenses.

---

## 1. Primary Databases

### UniProt Knowledgebase (UniProtKB)
- **Provider:** UniProt Consortium (EMBL-EBI, SIB, PIR)
- **Endpoint:** `https://rest.uniprot.org/`
- **Data Retrieved:** Amino acid sequences, accession codes, reviewed (Swiss-Prot) vs unreviewed (TrEMBL) status, protein families, allergen and anti-nutrient keywords.
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0).
- **Citation:** The UniProt Consortium (2023). UniProt: the Universal Protein Knowledgebase in 2023. *Nucleic Acids Res.* 51(D1):D523–D531.

### AlphaFold Protein Structure Database
- **Provider:** DeepMind & EMBL-EBI
- **Endpoint:** `https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/api/`
- **Data Retrieved:** 3D coordinate mmCIF/PDB files, per-residue pLDDT confidence scores.
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0).
- **Citation:** Varadi, M. et al. (2022). AlphaFold Protein Structure Database: massively expanding the structural coverage of protein-sequence space with high-accuracy models. *Nucleic Acids Res.* 50(D1):D439–D444.
