# Proti IQ Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 21, 2026  
**Jurisdiction:** Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) & Global Data Minimization Standards  

---

### 1. Data Minimization Principles
Proti IQ collects exclusively the minimum data required to personalize the alternative-protein discovery workspace and configure the Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) engine.

We collect:
- **Full Name:** Used solely to personalize document exports (Technical Data Sheets, Clinical Dietary Advisories, Recipe Cards).
- **Age:** Used strictly to determine dietary safety thresholds (e.g., activating Geriatric Sarcopenia Mode for users aged 50 and above to safeguard swallow viscosity and high-leucine requirements).
- **Geographic Location (Country and Region):** Used to configure local agricultural crop sourcing availability and lock market currency (NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR).
- **Mission and Facility Scale:** Used to deterministically lock your persona (Consumer, Nutritionist, Processor, Researcher).

### 2. What We Do NOT Collect
- No email addresses or passwords.
- No phone numbers.
- No payment card or banking details.
- No third-party tracking or behavioral profiling scripts.
- No medical history or biometric health records.

### 3. Data Storage & Third-Party Processing
- User profile data is stored securely in encrypted PostgreSQL databases hosted in ISO 27001-certified facilities.
- Chat queries are processed in real-time through Groq Cloud inference engines and are not used to train proprietary frontier AI models.
- Personal identifying information is never transmitted to LLM API prompts.

### 4. Right to Erasure (Data Deletion)
In compliance with NDPA Section 34 and international privacy regulations, you retain the permanent right to delete all your personal data at any time. When you trigger "Delete My Data", your record is immediately and irreversibly purged from all active database tables and cloud mirrors.
