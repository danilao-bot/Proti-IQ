### 1. Scientific & Information Scope
Proti IQ provides computational biophysical predictions, protein sequence comparisons, and general dietary formulation estimates based on Meta ESM-2 embeddings and published empirical wet-lab measurements.

### 2. Not Direct Medical Advice
Information delivered through the Clinical Nutritionist, Consumer, or Processor workspaces constitutes nutritional intelligence and formulation science, not individualized medical prescriptions. Clinical practitioners must exercise independent professional judgment before administering novel therapeutic diets.

### 3. Age Restrictions & Children's Privacy
Users must be at least 13 years of age to access Proti IQ. Individuals under 13 must use the platform only under the direct supervision of a parent, guardian, or qualified educator.

### 4. Commercial & Industrial Application
Industrial processing parameters (temperatures, shear pressures, coagulant dosages) are computational guidelines calibrated against seed storage globulin literature. Manufacturers are responsible for standard pilot-plant validation and national food safety compliance (e.g., NAFDAC, FDA, EFSA).

### 5. Fair Use & Platform Integrity
Automated scraping, denial-of-service attempts, prompt-injection attacks, and attempts to circumvent Role-Based Access Controls are strictly prohibited.
