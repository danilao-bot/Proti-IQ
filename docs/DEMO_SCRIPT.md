# Proti IQ — Pitch Script (7 Deliverables Structure)

**Hack the Fork Nigeria 2026 — Challenge 1**  
**Presenter Flow:** 3-Minute Live Walkthrough

---

### 1. Problem Definition (30s)
> *"Good day, judges. Nigerian food manufacturers and alternative-protein innovators face a major bottleneck: they want to formulate plant-based egg, milk, or tofu using local African crops like cowpea, Bambara groundnut, and pigeon pea. But lab testing every crop and extraction fraction is slow, expensive, and scattered across decades of academic papers with conflicting extraction protocols.*  
> *Without a clear screening tool, companies either default to imported soy or waste months in trial-and-error."*

---

### 2. Solution (30s)
> *"Enter **Proti IQ**: an in-silico decision-support platform designed specifically to rank indigenous Nigerian legume proteins for three functional jobs—plant-based egg, tofu-type gel, and plant milk—against proven global benchmarks like mung bean and soy.*  
> *In three clicks, a food developer sees which two or three local crops have the biochemical and structural likelihood to succeed in the lab first."*

---

### 3. AI & Analytical Component (35s)
> *"Proti IQ is not an LLM chatbot. It runs a rigorous biocomputational and machine learning pipeline:*  
> *First, it extracts biophysical features—isoelectric point, net charge, hydropathy, and cysteine fractions.*  
> *Second, it leverages deep protein language model embeddings—ESM-2 8M—to capture high-dimensional fold topology.*  
> *Third, it pairs structural data from AlphaFold DB with family-restricted pairwise alignment.*  
> *Every score is completely deterministic, explainable, and reveals the exact contribution of each biological driver."*

---

### 4. Evidence & Empirical Validation (35s)
> *"We do not ask you to take our word for it. We validate Proti IQ across two rigorous evidence tiers: directly measured single-lab wet-lab benchmarks and calibrated biophysical similarity.*  
> *In external literature tests, cowpea isolate closely mimics egg gel, and Proti IQ places cowpea squarely in the top tier alongside pigeon pea and groundnut.*  
> *Across single-lab empirical trials from the University of Pretoria, our pairwise comparisons show clear agreement on relative gelation order, while documenting intra-species variety spread—such as Cowpea Dr Saunders 5% versus Bechuana White 10%—as the experimental noise floor."*

---

### 5. User Value & Impact (25s)
> *"For a Nigerian food startup, Proti IQ cuts ingredient R&D cycles from six months down to two targeted lab trials. It keeps capital local, empowers African farmers by prioritizing indigenous crops, and accelerates clean-label food sovereignty."*

---

### 6. Limitations & Scientific Honesty (15s)
> *"We practice scientific discipline:*  
> *Scores are similarity-based likelihoods, not laboratory measurements.*  
> *Processing alters protein behavior, AlphaFold models are predictions, and crops with sparse sequence data carry explicit 'Low Confidence' badges."*

---

### 7. Next Steps & Road Ahead (10s)
> *"Our immediate next step is co-validating our top two candidate crops with a Nigerian food science faculty lab and integrating local extrusion and shear-processing data into the feature pipeline. Thank you!"*
